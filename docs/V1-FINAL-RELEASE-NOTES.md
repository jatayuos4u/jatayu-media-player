# Jatayu Media Player 1.0.0

**Your Media. Your World.**

Jatayu Media Player is a free and open-source audio and video player for Linux, created by **Anup Yadav**. It is an independent application, not preinstalled in JatayuOS.

## Highlights

- Audio and video playback powered by Qt 6 and libmpv
- Local media browsing, playlists, queues, music library and recent files
- Subtitle loading, track selection and playback controls
- Music metadata and cover-art editing
- Keyboard shortcuts, Help and preferences
- Ad-free, no built-in telemetry and no mandatory account
- Improved readability of dialogs on tested light and dark desktop themes

## Packages

- Debian-compatible amd64: **1.0.0-3** package
- Flatpak: `io.github.jatayuos4u.JatayuMediaPlayer`, KDE Platform 6.10 runtime

The corrected candidate was tested by the maintainer on Debian 13 KDE, Kubuntu and Lubuntu; updated Flatpak playback was also tested. Other distributions have not been comprehensively validated.
